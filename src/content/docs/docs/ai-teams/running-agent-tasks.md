## The Factory view

The Factory view lists every task with its state: waiting, running, verifying, blocked, done. Open a task to see the agent's timeline, its diffs and the commands it ran.

## The Agent Inbox

Everything that needs a person lands in one prioritized list: approvals, questions, escalated tasks, decisions and review requests. Approvals from every session are answered there.

## Verification reports

A verified task carries a report: what was checked, the commands and their output, and anything the verifier could not confirm. Read it before approving the merge.

## Snapshots

Before a risky step and before a merge, Vekrun keeps a snapshot of uncommitted work. Restoring is previewed, selective and undoable, and never touches files you have unsaved edits in. Changes outside the working copy, such as a database or a remote service, are not covered, and Vekrun says so.
