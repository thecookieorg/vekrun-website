## Describe the outcome

Open the Coordinator and say what needs to ship, in plain language: “Add CSV export to the monthly report, with the same filters as the table.” Pick the project if Vekrun cannot tell which one you mean.

The Coordinator may ask a question that changes the result. For larger work it proposes a plan you can read, edit and approve before anything runs.

## Follow the job

The job moves through its stages: investigating, implementing, testing, verifying and ready for review. You can pause it, redirect it, or take it over in the terminal at any point.

## When it needs you

Questions, approvals and blocks appear in the Inbox, each saying why it needs you and what happens next.
