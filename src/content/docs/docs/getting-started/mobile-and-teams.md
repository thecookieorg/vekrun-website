Vekrun Mobile, Keep Host Awake and Vekrun Teams are planned. Nothing on this page can be set up yet. See the [roadmap](/roadmap/#host-mobile).
:::

## Vekrun Mobile

An iOS and Android companion that pairs securely with your own Host, or your organization’s self-hosted Coordinator, over a private network you authorize. Assign work, follow jobs, answer the Inbox and read Proof Packs. No hosted Vekrun service is involved.

While the Host runs, **Keep Host Awake** prevents automatic idle sleep, even with no jobs, so it stays reachable. The screen can still turn off. Closing the lid, shutting down and critical battery stay under your operating system’s control.

## Vekrun Teams

A self-hosted Coordinator for your organization: a shared Board, Factory queues on your own workers, approvals, SSO, audit, memory and reporting. Engineers connect the same desktop app with your company SSO. See [Vekrun Teams](/teams/) for the plan and planned pricing.
