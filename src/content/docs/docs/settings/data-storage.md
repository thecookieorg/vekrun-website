Vekrun is local-first. Everything it keeps is on your disk, under your account.

## What is stored

- Settings, in layers: built-in defaults, your global settings, then per-project settings.
- Sessions and their timelines.
- Memories, with their scope and where each came from.
- Snapshots of uncommitted work taken before risky steps.
- A record of every permission decision and every action taken.
- Terminal layout and scrollback for restoring your session.

Nothing is sent to a Vekrun service. There is no account and no telemetry.

## Deleting data

Remove a provider to delete its key. Delete memories from the Memory panel. Snapshots have retention and storage controls.

:::note[Planned]
One-step deletion of a project's sessions, memories, indexes and downloaded models is planned, along with the search index itself. See the [roadmap](/roadmap/).
:::
