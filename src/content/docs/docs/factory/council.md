Verified on macOS only.

A Council is optional. No Factory task requires one.

## How a session runs

1. **Isolated first round.** Each member evaluates the question with its own model and without seeing the others, so the first positions are independent.
2. **Critique rounds.** Members critique the positions, not each other, and may revise.
3. **Moderator synthesis.** A moderator produces a report that separates verified evidence, assumptions, recommendations, uncertainties and unresolved risks, and records disagreements explicitly.
4. **Your decision.** The report ends with a decision for you to make. Agreement among members is not treated as proof.

Evidence cited in a report comes only from read-only tool results in the members' ledgers.

## Limits

Defaults are three members and two rounds. Each session shows an estimate before it starts and enforces its member count, round limit, budget and timeout. You can cancel at any time. Each member can use a different provider, and routing respects your privacy rules.
