## The idea

When you deliver work from Vekrun, the ticket moves in your tracker too, with your approval:

- A status change shows as done on the Board only after the tracker confirms it.
- Comments and the pull-request link are added where the service supports it.
- A retried update never posts the same comment twice.
- A conflict between what Vekrun knows and what the tracker has is shown to you rather than resolved silently.
